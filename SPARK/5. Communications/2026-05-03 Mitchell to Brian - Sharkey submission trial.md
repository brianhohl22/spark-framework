### Your Program Idea
- **Program/Concept Name:** Experimental School/Program for Echo Canyon
- **One Sentence Summary:** Develop a unique program at Echo Canyon as an experiment to see if there's enough momentum to resurrect Echo Canyon as a differentiable school-of-choice for SUSD families

**Detailed Description:**

One of the reasons Echo Canyon closed was that as a school-of-choice, the brand of that choice was not unique/attractive enough to bring in enough families. In the Arizona school choice market, SUSD needs to rely on both neighborhood schools and schools of choice to increase our enrollments across the district. Neighborhood schools are geographically determined. Schools of choice give us the potential leverage to recapture Scottsdale students whose families have chose non-SUSD schools. We have a few existing schools of choice (Cheynne Traditional, Pueblo and DC Dual Language, MSA). The availability of Echo gives us an excellent opportunity to build on our choice portfolio. The ask is to develop a program that has the potential to build sustainable, long-term growth of students at the Echo Canyon campus. Some criteria about the program:

- It should be unique from our other offerings (traditional, DLI, MSA), unless there's evidence that expanding one of those existing programs has sufficient demand. One example of a unique program is a "no screens" model that would emphasize reduction/elimination of devices as teaching tools and a focus on hands-on learning
- It should fit the geographic properties of Echo (its distance from other existing SUSD schools, its fit within Arcadia, its ability to pull from non-SUSD families to the west…)
- I realize that we just shut down Echo. This can be framed as a "rebranding" or "realignment" of Echo to focus the brand of program offered at Echo as a benefit for families. It's obvious what a DLI program is. Whatever we develop at Echo should have equal brand/positioning recognition
- This can be a staged/experimental program. We can start with a few grades (e.g. K-3) and expand later (e.g. K-5). There should be a path to full enrollment (e.g. a 3-year plan that looks at enrollment and staffing financially)
