Scottsdale Unified already offers dual language, gifted, IB, STEM, career, and traditional options. SPARK is the district team that looks after them and studies new ideas for learning environments. Every idea sent here gets a reply within 3 weeks. Each year SPARK selects two or three ideas for full study. This site keeps the record of ideas. People champion them in their school communities.

What happens to an idea

  1. You send it. We confirm within 2 school days.
  2. Within 3 weeks we tell you what's next. It moves to SPARK team review, joins a similar idea, or goes to a team that can act now. Or we hold it and say why.
  3. The SPARK team meets four times a year. In January it chooses two or three ideas to study in depth. Most ideas do not reach this step, by design.
  4. Everything studied, and what happened to it, is posted on What we're working on.
