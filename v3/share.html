Three quick questions

  1. Is it about something that already exists at one school? Talk to that school first. Every school has a SPARK contact, the principal or a designee. They can act locally or send the idea to SPARK for you. Ideas grow best with a champion at the school.
  2. Is it one of the ideas being studied now? Use the Support an idea form instead.
  3. Is it a concern about something happening now, such as a class, a bus, or a building? Use Let's Talk, SUSD's channel for questions and concerns. SPARK cannot act on these.

Not sure? Send it anyway. The SPARK team will route it.

Send your idea

You'll need your name, email, and connection to SUSD. Then a title, a few sentences on the idea, and why students and families want it. About 5 minutes. The form also asks its theme, what kind of idea it is, and whether it matches anything listed above.

What happens next

  1. Within 2 school days: you get an email with your idea's ID number.
  2. Within 3 weeks (15 school days): the SPARK screener replies with one of five outcomes.
    • Advanced: your idea goes to the next SPARK team review.
    • Merged: your idea joins a similar idea already listed; you get that idea's updates.
    • Redirected: another SUSD team can act on it now; we tell you who.
    • Outside SPARK's scope: we tell you why and where to take it.
    • Held: it fits SPARK, but we cannot take it up now. We tell you why and when we will look again.
    Ideas sent in June and July are reviewed in August.
  3. Quarterly SPARK team review: advanced ideas are scored. Each is selected for study, sent back to you for more detail, or archived with a reason. You are told either way.
  4. January: SPARK selects two or three ideas for full study that year. Results are presented in May and posted on this site.

Students: you can share an idea too. Ask a teacher or parent to help if you like.

We never publish your name or email. Your idea may appear on this site in summary form, in words written by the SPARK team.
