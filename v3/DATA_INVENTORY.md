## To confirm before launch

1. **AVID schools and the "schoolwide" label** (wording fixed in v3.1: the page lists SUSD's four schools and adds "some other schools also call themselves AVID schools; ask the school"; still confirm the list and the label) (#9, #10, #50, #51). The SPARK page and deck slide 7 list Tavan, Hohokam, Ingleside and Arcadia. Ingleside's own site does not mention AVID. The district AVID page says AVID "targets students in the academic middle", which pulls against the schoolwide label. Suggested fixes:
   - Elementary cell (#9): "AVID: Tavan, Hohokam (other schools: ask the school)".
   - Accordion (#51): "SUSD lists Tavan and Hohokam elementary, Ingleside Middle School, and Arcadia High School. Other schools, such as Yavapai, Copper Ridge, and Tonalea, also call themselves AVID schools."
2. **Tonalea and Copper Ridge AVID.** Tonalea's site says "Tonalea practices AVID strategies in all classes school wide". Copper Ridge (K-8) calls AVID "this school-wide program", but its middle grades also list AVID as an elective. Yavapai says "As an AVID school". None of the three is on the SPARK page or slide 7. Confirm whether SUSD counts them.
3. **Desert Canyon labels** (#60; fixed in v3.1 with the wording below). Slide 7 has a single entry, "Desert Canyon DLI". Suggested fix: "Mandarin path: Desert Canyon Elementary, Desert Canyon Middle School, and Desert Mountain High School. SUSD lists Desert Canyon's dual language as a signature program within a school." Keep "both" only if Educational Services confirms it.
4. **Gifted admission wording** (#90; fixed in v3.1 with the wording below). Suggested fix: "In the concept, all students would apply. Students already identified as gifted would be eligible automatically. Others would be reviewed on several measures, not one score."
5. **"The study continues"** (#93; removed in v3.1). Suggested fix: "The Board took no action. SUSD still lists it as a possible future theme." Also confirm that both concepts are still active in 2026-27. Slide 10 says 2026-27 team work is on "2-3 additional" concepts.
6. **Laguna or Laguna Prep** (#27, #76, #77). The STEM Certified Schools page says "Laguna Elementary School". The Our Schools page and the school's own site say "Laguna Prep".
7. **Cheyenne grades** (#30, #79). The Open Enrollment page says "K-8". The Traditional School page says "Pre-Kindergarten through Eighth Grade".
