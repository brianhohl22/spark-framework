This table lists every fact shown on the SPARK prototype pages in `docs/v3/`, where each fact comes from, and who should confirm it before launch. It covers programs.html (all main content), the board table, two study blocks, and the two status lines on working-on.html, the study names and status on index.html and share.html, and the "We are here" line on how-it-works.html. Example numbers (ideas by theme, "Already suggested") are illustrative and are not listed. Rows 1 to 105 were checked on **2026-09-23**, and rows 106 to 171 and the v3.2 changes on **2026-10-02**, against live SUSD web pages and the June 23, 2026 Governing Board record. **Status key:** Verified = a public SUSD source says it. Partly = part is sourced, part is not; the fix is in "To confirm before launch". Unsourced = no public source found. Example = marked on the page as illustrative, not a fact. Confirm = not posted yet, sources disagree, or no SUSD page states the contact route; the owner settles it before launch. Removed = no longer on the page. (date-bound) = the date rolls forward or is dropped at the content freeze.
