# SPARK site: feedback from the Sept 25 SPARK meeting, and what it means

For the Assistant Superintendent and the Web Content team. September 28, 2026. It maps each piece of feedback to the key consideration behind it, and to the suggested change. Reactions, different opinions, and better ideas are welcome. Website changes are specified in [V3.2_SPEC.md](V3.2_SPEC.md) (items W1 to W12). Items that aren't website changes (decisions, approvals, launch readiness, ongoing workflow) are noted in the last column.

## How we read the feedback

The site has three connected parts, and they serve every visitor all year:
- **What exists:** SUSD's programs.
- **What's coming:** ideas and concepts under study.
- **What's new:** share an idea, if it isn't already offered or being studied, and if it belongs with SPARK rather than another SUSD channel.

People move between the parts. Someone with an idea may find it already exists and become a supporter. A family exploring programs may care about what exists and about what's coming.

Seasons change how many people visit, not how the site is built. Open enrollment opens in early November (Nov 1, 2022; Nov 1, 2024; Nov 3, 2025), so more families will likely be exploring then. That shapes the launch date and which dates are current, not the layout.
