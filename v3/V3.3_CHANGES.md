# SPARK site v3.3: changes from v3.2 (October 26, 2026)

For the Web Content team and the SPARK site's author. This file continues [V3.2_SPEC.md](V3.2_SPEC.md). It lists changes W18 to W31, with final copy and placement. Everything not named here stays as in v3.2 and in [CONTENT.md](CONTENT.md).

**Where the changes come from.** In October 2026 the site's author asked three questions about Our programs: is it credible, can families find out how to enroll, and who do they contact? A research pass on SUSD's own pages (October 3) answered them. It also found that "What SPARK covers" overstated what a principal can decide alone. The site's author approved the candidate list on October 26, 2026: groups A (accuracy), B (credibility), and C (enrollment usefulness). This file is that list, written as build items.

**Status: built October 26, 2026.** Every fact added or changed was re-checked against live SUSD pages on 2026-10-26 ([DATA_INVENTORY.md](DATA_INVENTORY.md)). What the build found, and the QA results, are in [BUILD_REPORT.md](BUILD_REPORT.md).

**Rules that still apply** (from earlier decisions):
- Roles and offices only. No personal names, and no personal email addresses.
- "Programs" and "specialty schools" for what SUSD offers today. "Ideas" and "learning environments" only for new concepts. Never "options" as a name for programs.
- Never name candidate sites for new programs. Example and Planned items keep their visible badges. No invented data.
- **No claim of a lottery or of first come, first served.** SUSD's sources conflict: the open enrollment page says "Applications are processed as received", and Board policy JFB says "random selection". The site claims neither.
- **Phone numbers:** link to the page that lists them. Don't copy them onto the site (W30).
- **Dates roll forward** (V3.2_SPEC.md): at the content freeze, drop, reword, or roll forward any date that will be past on launch day.

**Element types used:** headings, paragraphs, bulleted lists, a two-column table that stacks on phones, accordions, and links. All are standard Finalsite Composer elements. [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) maps each new block.

## W18. Our programs: "Check for a seat" (A1)

**Where:** in "Applying and key dates". Delete the "Seats" bullet. Add an H3 "Check for a seat" (anchor `check-for-a-seat`) after the "How students join" block (W27). W28's list follows it.

**New copy:**

> **H3: Check for a seat**
>
> SUSD posts a seat list near the bottom of its [open enrollment page](https://www.susd.org/services/enrollment/open-enrollment), under the button "Open Enrollment Capacity." It shows each school as Open, Limited, or Closed. "Limited" means some grades have room.

**Why:** the old bullet said the list covers "each school and program" and is "updated regularly". The list is by school only. The file was last uploaded May 7, 2026 (rechecked October 26, 2026).

**Why a section, not a bullet:** A1 asked for a bullet. With C2's "What to ask the school" list it is too long for one bullet. A heading also gives it an anchor, which the Pueblo lines link to. Nothing in the copy depends on the form.

**Not on the page, on purpose:** that the list shows no date, and that the last upload is older than 12 weeks. The candidate list says to raise SUSD's own page issues privately, so the copy only says what the list shows and sends families to the registrar.

**Element:** Content (a heading and paragraphs).

## W19. How SPARK works: "What SPARK covers, and what it doesn't" (A2)

**Where:** replace the five bullets under that H2 (W8).

**New copy:**

> - **SUSD's specialty schools and programs.** These are on [SUSD's list](https://www.susd.org/our-district/district-committees/spark-team). Some are at one school, such as IB at Desert Mountain.
