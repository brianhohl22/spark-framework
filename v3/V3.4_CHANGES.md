# SPARK site v3.4: changes from v3.3 (October 28, 2026)

For the Web Content team and the SPARK site's author. This file continues [V3.3_CHANGES.md](V3.3_CHANGES.md). It lists changes W32 to W39. Everything not named here stays as in v3.3 and in [CONTENT.md](CONTENT.md).

**What v3.4 is.** Wording only, on Our programs and How SPARK works. No new facts and no new external links. The site will live on susd.org, so it should speak as the district, not about the district. The site's author approved this pass on October 28, 2026.

**Voice rules** (they apply to every page from now on):
1. State facts directly, and link the source. Don't write "SUSD says", "SUSD lists", "SUSD posts", or "SUSD uses". "SUSD" as a name is fine, as in "SUSD schools".
2. Say what is available and what to do next. Don't describe what other district pages leave out.
3. Answer stable questions (process, policy, dates) on the page. Send families to the school only for live, case-by-case answers, such as room in a grade today.
4. Name roles for what they do, not for who fills them.

**Status: built October 28, 2026.** [BUILD_REPORT.md](BUILD_REPORT.md) has the checks. [DATA_INVENTORY.md](DATA_INVENTORY.md) marks each changed row "v3.4".

## W37. Our programs: "Check for a seat"

**Was:**
> SUSD posts a seat list near the bottom of its open enrollment page, under the button "Open Enrollment Capacity." It shows each school as Open, Limited, or Closed. "Limited" means some grades have room.
>
> The list does not show grade levels. It does not show most programs, such as the Comprehensive Gifted Program, IB, or the Math and Science Academy. SUSD says it updates the list every 12 weeks.
>
> To check a grade or a program, call the school's registrar. Their phone numbers are on the same open enrollment page.
>
> What to ask the school: (five questions)

**Now:**
> The [open enrollment page](https://www.susd.org/services/enrollment/open-enrollment) has a seat list. Use the "Open Enrollment Capacity" button near the bottom of the page. It shows each school as Open, Limited, or Closed. "Limited" means some grades have room.
>
> For a specific grade or program, call the school's registrar. Each registrar's number is on the open enrollment page.
>
> For applications, tests, and deadlines, see [how students join](#how-students-join) and each program's key dates below.
>
> Questions to ask the registrar:
> - Is there room in my child's grade, or in the program?
> - If seats run short, how are students chosen?
> - Is there a waitlist? When will we hear?
> - Is there bus service from where we live?

**Why:**
- The paragraph about what the list does not show is gone. So is the update cadence. Enrollment owns the list, and the page now says what it shows and what to do next.
- The question about applications and deadlines is answered on this page, so it becomes a link.
- "Picked" became "chosen".
- The anchor `check-for-a-seat` is unchanged.

**Next step (not built):** answer the remaining questions on the page once Enrollment and Transportation confirm them: how students are chosen within a priority group, waitlist and notice steps, and bus service. The list would then become "Before you apply", with answers. The site still claims neither a lottery nor first come, first served.

## W39. How SPARK works: the school role

- **Was:** Role "School principal" (Planned: launch). Who: "The principal, or someone the principal names, at each school". What they do: "... Decides what is the school's to decide, such as clubs. Points other ideas to SPARK. ..."
- **Now:** Role "School SPARK contact" (Planned: launch). Who: "The principal, or a staff member the principal names, at each school". What they do: "Talks with the school community about ideas. Shares the link. Keeps school matters, such as clubs, with the principal, and points other ideas to SPARK. No forms, no reviewing."
- **Why:**
  - A role's name should say what it does, not who fills it.
  - If the principal names someone else, that person doesn't decide school matters. The principal does.
  - "Talk to the principal first" (Share an idea, question 1) and "Start with the principal" (How SPARK works) stay as they are, because those decisions are the principal's.
- **Still open:** whether the role is confirmed (DATA_INVENTORY.md, To confirm 33). Until then it keeps its Planned badge.
